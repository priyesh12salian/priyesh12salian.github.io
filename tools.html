Tools

Five small tools that run in your browser, one at a time. Nothing you enter leaves the page. Each tab has its own link you can share.

Project scope estimator

Tick what your NetSuite project needs and get an indicative effort range in weeks, with the engagement model I would suggest. Copy the summary and send it with your first message.

SuiteScript governance calculator

Enter what a script does per record and how many records it handles in one run. The calculator totals the governance units against the limit for that script type and tells you when to move the work to a Map/Reduce.

Integration map explorer

Every platform here has been connected to NetSuite in a project I delivered. Select one to see what moves, in which direction, and what runs on the NetSuite side.

Workflow or script?

Six questions about the automation you have in mind. The helper weighs the answers and suggests SuiteFlow, SuiteScript or a native feature, with the reasons and how I would build it.

NetSuite glossary

More than seventy terms that come up in NetSuite projects, each explained in a sentence or two. Type to filter, or pick a letter. Every term is also in the site search (⌘K / Ctrl K).
