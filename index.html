About

I'm a NetSuite Techno-Functional Consultant with 8.3+ years of hands-on experience across implementation, customisation, and integration. I've worked the full stack of the platform — SuiteBuilder, SuiteScript 1.0 to 2.1, SuiteFlow, SuiteAnalytics, SuiteBundler, and REST web services.

My work spans end-to-end EDI and intercompany implementations, procurement network and CRM integrations, manufacturing and WMS modules, custom approval workflows, and reporting built on saved searches, datasets, and SuiteQL. Lately I spend a lot of time pairing NetSuite with AI: agents that read invoices and purchase orders, and MCP-driven automations that act inside the ERP.

I also help teams adopt NetSuite well — training on standard practices on both the functional and technical side.
