Case study 4

Power BI dashboards over ODBC

Live finance dashboards in Power BI over ODBC, cutting manual reporting by about 40%.

  • Power BI
  • ODBC
  • SuiteQL / SQL
  • Finance

Problem

The problem

Leadership wanted live financial analytics, but every report was exported to spreadsheets and rebuilt by hand each period. Numbers were a week old by the time anyone saw them, and two people spent days producing them.

Step 1

Open a read-only pipe

SuiteAnalytics Connect exposes NetSuite over ODBC. A dedicated integration role with read-only access to the tables we needed keeps the connection safe and auditable.

Step 2

Author the datasets once

I wrote the SQL for balance sheet, income statement, AR and AP views against the ODBC schema, built them as Power BI datasets, and scheduled refreshes so the workbooks never need a manual pull.

Step 3

Dashboards instead of decks

Finance dashboards in Power BI with drill-down from statement line to transaction. The same datasets feed the monthly pack, so the deck and the dashboard can never disagree.

Result

The result

Real-time balance sheet, income statement, AR and AP dashboards. Manual reporting workload is down by about 40%, and the finance team spends the time on analysis instead of assembly.
