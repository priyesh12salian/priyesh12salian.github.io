Case study 3

Suitelet web apps for HR processes

Timesheets, payroll and leave handled in NetSuite-backed web apps, with no NetSuite licences needed.

  • Suitelet
  • Custom Records
  • Available Without Login
  • UI

Problem

The problem

Every employee who needed to log time or request leave needed a NetSuite licence and training. Licences were expensive, rollout was slow, and most people only needed three screens.

Step 1

A login that is not a NetSuite login

An available-without-login Suitelet handles sign-in against an employee custom record and issues a short-lived token. From there the user is in a plain web app, not the NetSuite UI.

Step 2

Four small apps

Dashboard, timesheet, payroll view and leave management, each a Suitelet with a simple responsive UI. Validation happens server-side in the Suitelet so the rules live in one place.

Step 3

Data lands in NetSuite

Every entry is written to custom records inside NetSuite, so approvals, reporting and payroll runs use the same system of record as finance. Nothing is synchronised, because nothing lives anywhere else.

Result

The result

Staff work in NetSuite-backed apps without NetSuite credentials or licences. HR and finance share one source of truth, and onboarding a new employee is a record, not a licence request.
